About me

"Both loveable and hateable,
sometimes questionable."

I'm a voice actor and indie VTuber based between Jakarta and Singapore, working in Indonesian and English. I landed Budi Raynar off a casting call somebody reposted onto my feed. Three games later, he's still mine.

My natural register sits somewhere between friendly and furious. Directors usually book me for characters who feel like real people having a very bad day.

Off the mic I stream to the #Abelive community on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X. I sing covers and play the games I've voiced, which is exactly as surreal as it sounds.
